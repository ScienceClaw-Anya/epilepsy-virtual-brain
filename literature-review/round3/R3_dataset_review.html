1. Executive summary

Five open datasets can carry the hackathon build.

  • VEC is the sandbox with a known EZ (30 virtual patients).
  • IDEAS + IDEAS II give anatomy, ready-made connectomes for 191 patients, resection masks for 433 and ILAE outcomes up to 5 years.
  • HUP ds004100 is the real-surgery check: 58 patients (57 with electrode coordinates), per-channel soz/resect labels and Engel outcomes.
  • Multi-Expert Seizure Annotation has consensus onset and spread labels for 78 SEEG seizures, 34 of them stimulation-induced, with the stimulation parameters.
  • PennEPI has 103 patients with raw iEEG, electrodes registered to their own parcellated MRI, and real treatments including RNS 12, DBS 3, VNS 3.

Four datasets from the original plan are not freely downloadable. Harvard EEG needs a credentialed BDSP DUA. The Ontario EpLink cohort is a controlled Brain-CODE release. MELD data are on request, and the shared data are surface features, not raw MRI. Epi25 is open only as gene and variant summaries; the individual exomes are in dbGaP, which holds 12,890 consented subjects, not the 20,979. Apply today if you want these, but don't build the demo on them.

No open dataset has everything. None combines a diffusion connectome, iEEG with coordinates, SOZ labels, resected tissue and outcome. IDEAS has connectomes and outcomes but no iEEG. HUP has iEEG and labels but no DWI. The practical bridge is a normative connectome for HUP; R2 found normative connectomes were not significantly worse (Proix 2017).

Two kinds of friction to plan for.

  • Formats and spaces: 4 signal formats (EDF, BrainVision, MEF3, NIfTI) and at least 5 coordinate systems or atlases (fsaverage, MNI152NLin6ASym, VEP-162, Lausanne, Desikan–Killiany). One shared atlas adapter will save every agent from re-solving this.
  • Leakage: six Penn sets probably share patients, and IDEAS shares 51 patients with ENIGMA.

2. T1 · Build: anatomy and connectomes

Inputs for constructing each patient's virtual brain: structural MRI, parcellations and, where available, diffusion connectomes, tied to what was later resected.

Formats your loaders must handle

  • EDF: ds004100, ds003876, HEEDB, TUH. BrainVision: ds003029, SWEC. MEF3: PennEPI (needs pymef). NIfTI + FreeSurfer: IDEAS, Bonn, Penn 3T–7T, PennEPI derivatives.
  • Every open set is BIDS (IDEAS, OpenNeuro, Pennsieve, NEMAR), so MNE-BIDS / pybids loaders cover most of the work. Brain-CODE and MELD formats could not be checked.

Leakage and overlap

  • Penn sets probably share patients: ds004100 (HUP IDs), PennEPI, Penn 3T–7T, Multi-Expert (HUP IDs), the seizure-severity set and the HUP normative atlas all come from one centre. Don't train on one and test on another without de-duplicating, for example by matching age, sex, implant type and target.
  • Other known overlaps: IDEAS shares 51 patients and 29 controls with ENIGMA. The HUP normative atlas merges MNI atlas data.

What decides the open contradictions

  • ds004100 coordinate space (README: ICBM152 MNI; coordsystem.json: fsaverage, metres). Plot a few contacts on both templates; whichever puts depth contacts in grey matter is right. R1 used fsaverage.
  • ds003029 coordinates (Bernabei 2023 says they were released; the v1.0.5 tree has no electrodes or coordsystem files). The tree decides, so treat ds003029 as EEG-only.
  • PennEPI SOZ labels: absent in the one patient opened. Open 2–3 more channels.tsv/events.tsv files before relying on PennEPI for onset scoring.
